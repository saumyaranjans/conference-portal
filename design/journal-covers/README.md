# Journal cover sources

The publisher artwork the covers in `public/journals/` were cut from. Kept
because the shipped covers are derived, not downloaded: each is cropped and
recomposed, and changing that crop means going back to these.

| Source | Shipped as | What was done |
|---|---|---|
| `fiib-source.png` | `fiib.jpg` | Masthead kept, section headings kept, article lists flood-filled with each block's own colour, volume line painted out |
| `ijabf-source.jpg` | `ijabf.jpg` | Roundel and title lifted from the masthead and restacked on the journal's yellow |
| `rml-source.jpg` | `rml.jpg` | Cropped to the series band and book title, stopping above "VOL 5" |

They are issue covers, so all three carry a volume/issue line that must not
reach the site — the covers stand for the outlet, not for one issue.

Kept out of `public/` deliberately: at 964KB together they are ~20x the weight
of the covers actually served, and nothing should link to them.
