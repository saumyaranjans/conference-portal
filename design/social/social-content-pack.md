## 2. Hashtags

**How many.** Instagram: 8–12, and put them on the first comment rather than in
the caption if you want the caption to read cleanly — reach is identical.
LinkedIn: 3–5, inside the post. LinkedIn actively suppresses hashtag-stuffed
posts, so the temptation to paste the whole bank is worth resisting.

**The rule that matters.** Always include the three core tags. Then pick from
whichever groups the post is actually about. A tag that does not describe the
post costs you reach rather than gaining it, because the people it reaches
bounce.

### P2 — Theme explainer (week of 25 August)

**LinkedIn**

```
Three words in our 2027 theme, and why each is there.

FLEXIBILITY. Adaptability is not the same as automation. An organisation that has automated a rigid process has a faster rigid process. The flexible systems tradition asks what actually lets an enterprise change its mind — and AI can either widen that capacity or quietly narrow it.

DIGITALISATION. The interesting problems have stopped being about model accuracy. They are about what happens when a recommendation meets an accountable human being who must defend the decision afterwards. Governance, not accuracy, is increasingly the binding constraint on adoption.

DECARBONIZATION. AI is being asked to help decarbonise operations and supply chains while itself consuming meaningful energy. Both halves of that ledger belong in the same conversation.
```

## 5. Practical notes

**Cross-post to IIM Sambalpur.** The institute's channels have an audience this
account has to build from nothing. Ask the PR & Media Committee to reshare P1,
P4, P8 and P9 — the four that matter most for submissions.

- Facebook: facebook.com/IIMSBP
- X: x.com/iim_sambalpur
- Instagram: instagram.com/iim_sambalpur
- LinkedIn: linkedin.com/school/indian-institute-of-management-sambalpur
- YouTube: youtube.com/@PRMediaCommitteeIIMSambalpur

**Tag the GIFT Society** on every call-for-papers post once you have the handle.

**Post timing** for an Indian academic audience: 10:00–11:00 or 19:00–21:00 IST,
weekdays. LinkedIn is dead at the weekend.

**Answer the comments.** Most will be "is it hybrid?", "what is the fee?" and
"can PhD students present?" — all yes, and all answerable in one line. An
unanswered question on a call-for-papers post costs you submissions.

**Watch the referrers.** The portal's Visit Analytics now records where traffic
comes from, so you will see which of these posts actually drove people to the
site. Use it after P4 to decide what to repeat.

**Do not post** speaker names, award winners or attendance numbers before they
are confirmed. Corrections travel less well than the original claim.
