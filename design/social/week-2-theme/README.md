## LinkedIn copy

Three words in our 2027 theme, and why each is there.

FLEXIBILITY. Adaptability is not the same as automation. An organisation that
has automated a rigid process has a faster rigid process. The flexible systems
tradition asks what actually lets an enterprise change its mind — and AI can
either widen that capacity or quietly narrow it.

DIGITALISATION. The interesting problems have stopped being only about model
accuracy. They are about what happens when a recommendation meets an
accountable human being who must defend the decision afterwards. Governance,
not accuracy, is increasingly the binding constraint on adoption.

DECARBONIZATION. AI is being asked to help decarbonise operations and supply
chains while itself consuming meaningful energy. Both halves of that ledger
belong in the same conversation.

what is already rigid?

Digitalisation: who remains accountable when an AI recommendation becomes a
management decision?

energy it consumes?
